  GOAL: A standalone, fully-populated NHL data warehouse feeding an explainable
  Bayesian scoring/projection model tuned to my Yahoo league. The model's whole
  point is a VARIANCE DESCRIPTION per player — floor / ceiling / confidence and
  head-to-head P(A > B) — NOT a single-point projection. It must be re-runnable
  daily to condition on games already played and sharpen rest-of-season value.

  === THE MODEL (the heart of this) ===
  The existing model's `game_level_modelling.py` is a hierarchical Bayesian
  state-space model in PyMC and is the RIGHT foundation — keep its architecture:
  - Player latent scoring rate as a GaussianRandomWalk across seasons (drifts year
    to year — captures breakout/decline, e.g. a player who's elite one year and
    injury-hit the next).
  - Opponent/team strength as an AR(1) process per team across seasons.
  - Home/away effect; Poisson likelihoods for count stats.
  - Posterior predictive over the REMAINING schedule → full distribution of
    rest-of-season totals; condition on games already played so a daily re-run
    updates the projection.
  (Ignore the older `model.py` — it's an inferior linear regression. Also note the
  old `data_prep_bayesian.py` fakes PPP=PPG and SHP=SHG, which is wrong.)

  DO NOT switch away from the Bayesian paradigm — it's chosen deliberately because
  point-estimate models can't give calibrated floor/ceiling/confidence. You MAY
  add a non-Bayesian model (e.g. NGBoost / quantile gradient boosting) ONLY as a
  calibration benchmark, never as the primary.
